# AD9361

The AD9361 is a wide-band RF agile transceiver from Analog Devices with integrated TX/RX chains, useful in custom SDR and software-defined radio designs. It is popular in research radios and prototyping stacks where configurable bandwidth, tuning, and gain chain control are needed.

---

## ⚙️ Overview

The chip is dual-channel capable in some deployment modes and supports a large frequency range with programmable digital baseband path settings. Teams often pair it with FPGA or SoC hosts for custom PHY logic and protocol experimentation.

For agent-driven parameterized CAD/flight loops, AD9361 is usually chosen when you need radio behavior as a tunable subsystem, not a fixed module.

---

## 🧠 Core Capabilities

- Wide tuning range and flexible local oscillator setup.
- Programmable filters and gains for custom link budgets.
- Multiple interface patterns to host logic:
  - SPI for register/program control,
  - LVDS/parallel interfaces for high-throughput I/Q paths depending on platform,
  - clock/reset controls.
- Suitable for custom PHY and waveform implementation.

---

## 📊 Practical Comparison (AD9361 vs integrated modules)

| Property | AD9361 + host stack | Integrated RC modules |
|---|---|---|
| RF flexibility | Very high | Medium |
| Development effort | High | Low |
| Determinism control | Very high | Vendor-defined |
| Time-to-prototype | Medium | High-quick |
| Best usage | Research / custom protocols | Fielded RC or fixed stack products |

---

## 🛠 Development Notes

- Most production workflows use a Linux/FPGA host and IIO/drivers.
- For scripted loops, treat radio config as part of the experiment graph:
  - center frequency,
  - sample rates,
  - TX gain profile,
  - RX gain profile,
  - interpolation/decimation chain settings.
- Keep configuration scripts source-controlled; they become part of reproducibility.

---

## 🔧 Integration Context

- **Open-source ecosystems**: used in custom SDR stacks and academic waveform prototyping.
- **UAV experimentation**: useful for custom C2 links or resilience experiments where standard RC modules are too restrictive.
- **CI for radio tuning**: config vectors can be fed from JSON/YAML in repeatable test grids.
