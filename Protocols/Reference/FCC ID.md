# FCC ID

`FCC ID` (often written `FCCID`) means **FCC Identifier** — the identification code used by the U.S. FCC for equipment authorization of radio-frequency emitting devices.

---

## What it is

Under FCC rules, many transmit/receiver products and other RF equipment require certification and labeling with a valid FCC ID.

The identifier is composed of:

- **grantee code** (assigned to manufacturer/applicant),
- **product code** (assigned by grantee for a specific product/model filing).

When combined, this identifies an equipment authorization filing and the technical record tied to that hardware.

---

## Why it is useful to engineers

A valid FCC ID often lets you find:

- approved filing entry,
- test reports and measurement context,
- external photos,
- internal photos (sometimes available),
- manuals, test setup, and related exhibits.

That can replace missing vendor documentation when troubleshooting or designing replacements.

---

## How to look one up fast

1. Locate the marking on the device/label: `FCC ID`/`FCC` + code.
2. Search the code in an FCC equipment-authorization database.
3. Open the filing record and inspect exhibits for teardown/measurement clues.
4. Cross-check model/manufacturer and revision against the physical board before reuse.

---

## Practical caveat

Not every internal document is always public immediately; some exhibits can be under confidentiality windows and appear later.
